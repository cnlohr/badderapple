Exponential-Golomb Encoding
  • Use fewer bits for smaller numbers.
  • Use more bits for bigger numbers.
  • Easy to encode/decode.
  • No need to store a table!
  • Extensively used in H.264 and H.265
K-means
  • Math PhDs
  • Scales to 64-dimensions well
  • Easily tuneable, for more or less clusters
  • Very fast

VPX or "Binary Range Coding"
  • b = a bit (0 or 1)
  • prob = probability of a 0.
    • integer, 0..255 out of 256
  • When encoding: vpx_write(w, b, prob);
  • When decoding: b = vpx_read(w, prob);
  • github.com/cnlohr/vpxcoding
  • codeberg.org/cnlohr/speedtropy
  • Can be arbitrary.
  • Probabilities can change per-read.
  • VPX is a "special" case of range coding.
    • VPX from google's VP8, VP9 video codecs.
